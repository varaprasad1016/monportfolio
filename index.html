01 — About

Analytics that people actually use

I'm a data analyst at Neatcom Telematics in Coventry, where I build and maintain the SQL and Power BI layer that the operations and sales teams rely on — the reporting, the KPI dashboards, and the pipelines feeding them.

My background sits between analytics and applied machine learning: an MSc in Data Science from Coventry University, the IBM Data Science Professional Certificate, and project work in deep learning for medical imaging. I'm most useful where a messy dataset has to become a number somebody can act on.

Beyond the queries, I care about the parts that make analysis stick: clean data, clear definitions, and a dashboard a stakeholder can read without a walkthrough.

  • Reporting & BIPower BI and advanced Excel dashboards built around the KPIs a team is measured on.
  • SQL & data engineeringQuery optimisation, data extraction and ingestion pipelines that hold up under load.
  • Machine learningClassification, regression and deep learning in Python, from development to deployment.
  • Working across teamsTranslating between operations, sales and the data, so the output answers the real question.
